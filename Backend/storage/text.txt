🇮🇳 Independence Day of India 🇮🇳

India celebrates Independence Day every year on **15th August** to remember the day when our country became independent from British rule in **1947**.

This day reminds us of the courage, sacrifices, and struggles of the freedom fighters who dedicated their lives to India's freedom. 🇮🇳🫡

Independence Day is celebrated across the country with great enthusiasm. The **National Flag 🇮🇳** is hoisted, the national anthem is sung, and people participate in various cultural programs and activities.

Independence is not only about freedom from foreign rule. It also means having the responsibility to make our country better through education, honesty, unity, and respect for others. 🤝📚

As young citizens, we should contribute to the progress of our nation and respect the values for which our freedom fighters struggled.

**Happy Independence Day! 🇮🇳❤️
Jai Hind! 🇮🇳
Vande Mataram! 🙏**
